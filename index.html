Our Story

A passion born from wandering the world

Our love for delicious, healthy food, exquisite desserts, and extraordinary beverages inspired us to create Evergreen. We spent nearly two years traveling the globe, seeking the finest recipes and curating an unparalleled selection for our restaurant.

The result is a menu that weaves together French pastry artistry, Georgian warmth, Mediterranean freshness, and Eastern European soul — all presented in a space designed for harmony with nature.

What we offer

Dining Room

Tables crafted from a 200-year-old maple slab, stoneware from Villeroy & Boch, natural moss and Belgian greenery creating an atmosphere of unity with nature.

Mountain Patio

An extension of our concept into the open air — premium outdoor furniture, pleasant shade, ambient music, and stunning mountain views complete the experience.

Pet Friendly Dining

And we are extremely welcoming to guests with pets, for whom we always have free treats and a bowl of water. Please do not leave your friends at home while you enjoy visiting our restaurant. They deserve to visit us too, and we appreciate and understand this.
